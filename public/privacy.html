Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Evergreen Heritage Communities ("we", "us", or "our") respects your personal privacy. As a family-owned and operated housing company, we collect only the minimal information necessary to assist prospective residents, answer inquiries, and maintain our properties for existing residents.

2. Information We Collect

Through our public website, we collect only voluntarily submitted contact information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Homesite or Community of Interest
  • Resident Maintenance Request details and optional uploaded photos
No Sensitive PII Collected Online:
We do NOT ask for or collect Social Security numbers, bank account logins, or driver's license copies through this standard website form. Formal tenant background and credit screenings are handled through dedicated, secure, encrypted tenant verification portals or in person at our management offices.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted on this site is used solely to:

  • Respond to questions and schedule tours of our manufactured home communities.
  • Notify waitlisted individuals when a matching home or homesite becomes available.
  • Dispatch maintenance personnel to address repair requests submitted by residents.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or telemarketers.

4. Data Security

All website traffic is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (HTTPS). Submitted maintenance photos and contact requests are securely stored with restricted access limited to authorized management personnel.

5. Contacting Us Regarding Your Data

If you wish to update, review, or remove your contact information from our prospective resident list, simply email us at info@evergreencommunities.com or call (360) 890-3401.
