Family Owned Since 1989

About Evergreen Heritage Communities

For over 35 years, our family has owned and operated clean, dependable, and neighborly manufactured home communities across the state of Texas.

The Miller Family Journey

Our Story & Heritage

In the late 1980s, Robert and Martha Miller recognized that quality, affordable housing was slipping out of reach for everyday working families and retirees. Believing that manufactured housing provided the ideal foundation for comfortable independence, they purchased their first small mobile home community in Central Texas in 1989.

From day one, Robert and Martha chose to do things differently. Instead of treating the park as an absentee financial asset, they repaved roads, planted mature shade trees, installed reliable underground utilities, and lived on-site to handle repairs personally.

Today, three generations of the Miller family are actively involved in day-to-day operations. We now operate four communities across Round Rock, Tyler, New Braunfels, and Denton, providing homesites to over 500 Texas families.

What Guides Us

Our Core Management Philosophy

We hold ourselves to straightforward principles that build trust, stability, and neighborhood pride.

Resident Dignity & Respect

We treat every homeowner and tenant with kindness, courtesy, and fairness. Everyone deserves a safe, well-kept, and peaceful environment to call home.

Honest & Transparent Pricing

No sudden surprise fees or predatory lease terms. We provide clear, itemized statements, fair annual adjustments, and transparent utility pass-throughs.

On-Site Professional Management

We don't outsource to distant corporate management companies. Our on-site managers and maintenance staff are full-time employees who know our residents personally.

Long-Term Generational Focus

We are long-term stewards, not real-estate flippers. We continuously reinvest rental income back into street paving, water infrastructure, and recreational spaces.
