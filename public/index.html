Our Heritage

About Our Family Business

In 1989, Robert and Martha Miller purchased their first manufactured home community in Washington State with a simple philosophy: treat residents like neighbors, take meticulous care of the property, and provide honest, dependable management.

Over the past 35+ years, our family business has grown to four residential communities while keeping those core founding values unchanged. Today, the second and third generations of the Miller family work directly alongside our on-site community managers.

When you choose an Evergreen Heritage community, you aren't dealing with an out-of-state corporate conglomerate. You're partnering with a Northwest family that takes deep personal pride in keeping our neighborhoods clean, safe, and peaceful for generations of residents.
